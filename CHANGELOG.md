# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-26

First public release.

- Live map of every connection, on a 3D globe with day and night or on flat maps (standard, muted, satellite, hybrid).
- Traffic per app, process, domain, place and country, with rates, volumes, ports, protocols and latency.
- Real domain names from DNS answers, through an optional helper.
- Trackers from DuckDuckGo's lists and clear-text connections flagged, with a warning on open Wi-Fi.
- Weekly recap, journal of first times, day replay and network diagnosis.
- ⌘K search, menu bar item, desktop widget, mobile data tracking, CSV and JSON export, Shortcuts actions.
- English and French.
